On test day the officer asks up to 20 of these 128 questions and stops after 12 correct answers.

Questions tagged verify depend on who holds office or where you live. Check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates in the week of your interview.

Each day's set favors questions you missed, then ones you have not seen, then the ones you answered correctly longest ago. Changing the number of questions per day adds to or trims today's set; questions you already answered stay.
